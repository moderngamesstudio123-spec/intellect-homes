THE INTELLECT JOURNEY

Your 6-Step Path To Stress-Free Handover

From initial council feasibility to the day we hand over your champagne and keys.

01

Site & Council Feasibility

We review your block slope, soil conditions, council overlays (heritage, bushfire, flood), and underground service assets before you commit a single dollar.

02

Architectural Concept & 3D

Collaborate with our in-house designers to fine-tune your floor plans, facade textures, solar orientation, and bespoke spatial layout in photorealistic 3D.

03

Fixed-Price Tender & Contract

Receive an exhaustive, itemised HIA or MBA building contract. Every tapware code, tile finish, and structural specification is locked down in stone.

04

Permits, Demolition & Engineering

We handle all council town planning, building permits, utility disconnections, asbestos audits, and civil engineering without you having to lift a finger.

05

Master Construction & Audits

Your dedicated Site Supervisor oversees each phase: Base Slab, Frame, Lockup, Fixing, and Final. Independent building surveyor certificates at each milestone.

06

Handover & 10-Yr Warranty

Enjoy your celebratory handover day, complete with appliances manuals, maintenance warranties, and ongoing post-handover customer support.
